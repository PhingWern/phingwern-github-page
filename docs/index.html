Privacy Policy

PWT POS System (“App”, “we”, “us”)

Effective date: September 12, 2026 · Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy describes how the PWT POS System application handles information. The App is designed to run primarily on your device using a local database. We do not operate a general cloud account system or sell personal data.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to users of the PWT POS System application (Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux, and related builds) published under package / application identifiers associated with this project (for example, com.posapp.pwt_pos_system).

2. Information the App stores on your device

Business and operational data you enter is stored locally on the device (typically in a SQLite database and app preferences), including:

  • Staff accounts (names, roles, PIN-based credentials)
  • Products, variants, categories, suppliers, and related inventory data
  • Orders, payments, discounts, cart drafts, and sales history
  • Expenses and report-related records you create
  • Receipt and store settings (business name, address, tax, logo, printer preferences)
  • Optional product images you attach from the camera or photo library
  • App preferences such as language and enabled modules

This information stays on the device unless you export, share, print, back up, or transfer it using features you choose to use.

3. Permissions and device features

Depending on platform and features you use, the App may request:

  • Bluetooth — to discover and connect to thermal receipt printers you select.
  • Location (Android) — only as required by the operating system for Bluetooth device scanning. The App does not use your location for tracking, advertising, or mapping.
  • Camera / photos — only when you choose to add or change a product image or similar media.
  • Internet / network — for optional features described below (not for uploading your full sales database to our servers).
  • Printing / sharing — when you print or share receipts, labels, or exported reports through the system share sheet or printer services.

4. Network / online processing

The App may use the network in limited cases:

  • Character encoding conversion — when printing certain characters (for example Chinese text on some thermal printers), the App may send those characters to a conversion API to obtain printer-compatible encodings. This request is for conversion only; it is not intended to transmit full customer profiles or your entire database.
  • Remote images you configure — if you set a logo or image URL, the App may fetch that URL to display or print it.
  • Development / update infrastructure — standard OS and store mechanisms may communicate with Apple, Google, or other platform services.

We do not currently use third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs to track end users across apps or websites.

5. How information is used

Information on the device is used to:

  • Operate the point-of-sale features you enable (sales, inventory, reports, receipts)
  • Authenticate staff with local PINs and roles
  • Print or share receipts and labels you request
  • Remember your settings and preferences

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal or business data. Data may leave the device only when:

  • You print, share, export, or back up data
  • You connect a Bluetooth printer you choose
  • Limited conversion or image-fetch requests described above are made
  • Required by law or to protect rights and safety

If you are a merchant using the App, you are responsible for how you handle customer personal data under applicable laws (for example, what you print on receipts or store in order notes).

7. Data retention and deletion

Because data is stored on your device, retention is controlled by you. Uninstalling the App, clearing app data, or deleting records inside the App removes or reduces that local data according to the platform and feature used. We do not maintain a separate cloud copy of your POS database for this App.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for business / retail use and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

9. Security

We rely on device storage protections provided by the operating system and local access controls (such as staff PINs). No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Protect physical access to devices that run the App and use strong device lock screens.

10. International users

If you use optional network features, processing may occur on servers in other regions (for example, cloud functions used for encoding conversion). Local business data remains on your device unless you transfer it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
